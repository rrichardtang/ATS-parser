# title: Machine Learning Engineer, Applied
# level: mid
Take models from notebook to production and keep them healthy there.

Requirements
- 2-4 years applied machine learning in production settings.
- Python, PyTorch, and the HuggingFace transformers ecosystem.
- Feature engineering and data pipelines at scale: Spark, SQL, dbt or Airflow.
- Model deployment, versioning and rollback; CI/CD for model artifacts.
- Experiment tracking with Weights & Biases or MLflow.
- Monitoring for data drift, model drift and performance regression.
- You can state the metric your model moved and by how much, on what dataset.
- Comfortable with A/B testing and offline/online metric correlation.
Nice to have: recommender systems, ranking models, XGBoost, ONNX, Triton,
Kubeflow, feature stores.
