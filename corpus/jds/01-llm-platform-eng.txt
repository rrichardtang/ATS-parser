# title: AI Engineer, LLM Platform
# level: mid
Build and operate the serving path for large language model features in production.
You will own inference latency, throughput and cost for models behind user-facing
products, and the evaluation harness that decides whether a change ships.

Requirements
- 2-5 years building production software in Python.
- Hands-on experience serving transformer models: vLLM, TGI, Triton or equivalent.
- Comfort with GPU memory, batching strategy, quantization and KV cache behaviour.
- Experience with FastAPI or similar for wrapping inference behind an API.
- Docker and Kubernetes for deployment; familiarity with AWS, GCP or Azure.
- You have measured p50/p95/p99 latency and throughput and improved them.
- Experience designing offline evaluation: golden datasets, regression suites,
  LLM-as-judge pipelines, and human review workflows.
- Monitoring and observability for model behaviour: drift, hallucination rate,
  cost per request, token usage.
Nice to have: fine-tuning experience with LoRA or QLoRA, PyTorch internals,
CUDA profiling, experience with Ray or Spark for offline batch inference.
