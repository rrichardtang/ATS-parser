# title: AI Engineer, Evaluation
# level: mid
Decide whether models are good enough to ship, and build the systems that answer that.

Requirements
- 3+ years engineering with strong Python.
- Designing evaluation harnesses for generative systems: golden datasets, rubrics,
  LLM-as-judge with calibration against human labels.
- Statistical literacy: sample size, confidence intervals, significance, variance
  between runs.
- Experience catching regressions before release; CI gating on eval metrics.
- Annotation operations and quality control.
- Measuring groundedness, faithfulness, hallucination rate, refusal behaviour,
  and toxicity or safety metrics.
Nice to have: RAGAS, LangSmith, Braintrust, Inspect, red-teaming, adversarial
prompt generation, benchmark design.
