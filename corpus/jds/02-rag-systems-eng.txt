# title: AI Engineer, Retrieval
# level: mid
Own retrieval quality for our knowledge products. Building a RAG demo is easy;
this role is about one that survives messy data, ambiguous queries and corpus drift.

Requirements
- 3+ years of software engineering, strong Python.
- Production RAG experience: chunking strategy, embedding model selection, hybrid
  search combining BM25 with dense retrieval, and reranking with a cross-encoder.
- Vector database experience: pgvector, Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant or FAISS.
- You evaluate retrieval separately from generation. You know recall@k, MRR and
  nDCG and you measure them before touching the prompt.
- Experience versioning embedding models and planning re-embeds.
- SQL and data pipeline experience; Airflow, Dagster or similar.
- Prompt engineering and structured output: function calling, JSON schema, tool use.
Nice to have: agent frameworks, LangChain or LlamaIndex, LangSmith or RAGAS for
tracing and evaluation, multilingual retrieval, ColBERT-style late interaction.
