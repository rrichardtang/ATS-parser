# title: Founding AI Engineer
# level: mid
Small team, broad scope. You will build features, serve models, and own reliability.

Requirements
- 3+ years shipping production software; Python and TypeScript.
- Experience integrating LLM APIs and self-hosted open-weight models.
- RAG pipelines end to end: ingestion, chunking, embeddings, retrieval, reranking.
- FastAPI, Postgres, pgvector, Redis, Docker; deploying on AWS or GCP.
- Evaluation discipline: you do not ship prompt changes without measuring them.
- Cost management: token spend, caching, model selection by task.
- Comfort with ambiguity, and evidence of shipping to real users.
Nice to have: fine-tuning, vLLM, agent frameworks, observability tooling,
frontend work in React.
