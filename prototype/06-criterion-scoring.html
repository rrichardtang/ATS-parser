What is a category worth, when two judges answered its criteria?

Ticket 06 of the rubric-migration map. Until ticket 05 the content judge returned a number per category, and two judges were combined by averaging their numbers. Since 05 the judge answers five yes/no criteria per category and names no number at all; the band, and the band's value, are looked up from the answers. Nothing yet turns those answers into a score. This page is the three decisions that replacement needs, made pressable: how two judges who split on a criterion are combined, what the report says when they do, and what a withheld category does to the composite.
