A recruiter scanning for seconds, then a hiring manager asking whether the work is real. These fail for unrelated reasons, so they are scored separately.

Most ATSs do not auto-reject on resume content — people do. So the question is not how to beat a filter; it is whether the file parses, whether a search surfaces you, and whether a human is convinced.
