# Band probes

Seven short resumes written for ticket 05, each aimed at one band or at the boundary
between two. They exist because `tests/fixtures/` cannot exercise this rubric: four of
its seven carry the same bullets, three carry almost nothing, and every one that can be
answered at all lands in band A or band E. Three of the five bands were never reached.

These are plain text, not PDFs, and they live here rather than in `tests/fixtures/`
because they test the *rubric*, not the parser. Extraction is not what they are for —
they are handed to the probe already readable, so a criterion disagreement is about the
criterion.

Format is the minimum `ats.sections.parse` needs: a role heading with a date range,
then bullets.
